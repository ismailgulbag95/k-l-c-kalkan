# Kül Çemberi — app icon identity brief / round 1

**Status:** concept review; no final mark or platform icon approved yet.

## Working brief

- **Name:** Kül Çemberi (the working title already used in the game plan).
- **Product:** an original, offline, turn-based mobile arena RPG set in a storm-swept island league.
- **Audience and use:** mobile RPG players; the symbol must read at 16–32 px, survive an app-icon crop, and work in one colour on light and dark backgrounds.
- **Assumed traits:** fierce, weathered, tactical, and clear at a glance.
- **Avoid:** crossed swords, generic shields, laurel wreaths, copied Swords and Sandals characters/art, fine text, and a literal Roman-colosseum seal.
- **Decision maker:** user. These assumptions can be revised after the first concepts.

## Word map

Kül → cinder, fractured basalt, ember, residue. Çember → arena ring, boundary, contest. Storm-swept island league → broken horizon, tide, wind, ascent. The strongest intersections are a ring breached by an ember, the working-title initial K formed as an arena gate, and a cinder emerging between basalt shards.

## Concept longlist and quick score

Scores are 1–5 for idea clarity / distinction / simplicity / relevance / small-size strength.

1. **Kül Halkası:** a near-complete arena ring opens around a rising spark. Abstract. **4 / 4 / 5 / 5 / 5**.
2. **Kül K:** a custom K uses a gate-like spine and two widening cuts. Letterform. **5 / 3 / 5 / 4 / 5**.
3. **Bazalt Kıvılcımı:** two broken basalt pieces frame a single rising ember. Pictorial. **4 / 4 / 4 / 5 / 4**.
4. Four tournament steps fold into one crown-like ember.
5. A tide line becomes the crossbar of a C.
6. A wind-carved notch splits an arena floor into two halves.
7. A single cinder sits inside a square cut in a black ash field.
8. Four separated stones imply the four campaign circuits without enclosing the mark.
9. A broken horizon rises into a narrow flame, representing the island league's climb.
10. A round arena boundary is interrupted by a directional gap that points toward the next bout.

The first three are the clearest and most different mark types for this round. Kül Halkası is the recommendation because it carries the arena/ash idea in one compact silhouette without borrowing a character, weapon, or familiar heraldic badge. Its open ring must be checked against nearby arena-game icons before final use.
